## MARKET RESEARCH
For your reference only
 
**1. Fundamentals of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu by Rener and Ryron Gracie (Gracie University Online)** — Gracie University (gracieuniversity.com) · $197-$297/year
- Why it sells: Highly structured beginner curriculum from the most recognized BJJ family name, includes video lessons with testing and belt progression tracking, giving beginners a clear roadmap which reduces overwhelm
- Gap: Expensive for true beginners unsure of commitment, limited live sparring context, and some users feel the self-defense focus diverges from sport BJJ that most gyms teach
 
**2. Dynamic Striking MMA Fundamentals Course** — Udemy · $15-$30 (frequent sales)
- Why it sells: Low price point removes financial risk for beginners, covers striking basics for MMA in digestible video modules, accessible on mobile, and Udemy's algorithm pushes it to fitness-curious buyers
- Gap: Production quality varies, no feedback mechanism or community, content can feel generic and disconnected from real gym training environments
 
**3. John Danaher's Go Further Faster Series (Leg Locks, Guard Passing, etc.)** — BJJ Fanatics (bjjfanatics.com) · $97-$177 per volume
- Why it sells: Danaher is considered the top BJJ coach in the world, his systematic and conceptual teaching style resonates strongly, and BJJ Fanatics has massive email list and affiliate marketing reach
- Gap: Content depth is often better suited for intermediate players despite beginner marketing, high price per volume adds up quickly, and the lecture-heavy format can lose impatient beginners
 
**4. Flo Grappling Subscription (FloGrappling)** — FloGrappling (flograppling.com) · $12.99-$29.99/month
- Why it sells: Offers live and on-demand competition footage plus instructional content in one subscription, beginners are motivated by watching high-level matches to emulate techniques, strong brand in the grappling community
- Gap: Not structured as a beginner curriculum, overwhelming content library with no guided learning path, and beginners may struggle to extract actionable lessons from competition footage alone
 
**5. Stephan Kesting's Roadmap for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu** — Grapplearts.com / direct download · $37-$67
- Why it sells: Specifically designed for white belts, offers a prioritized learning sequence that solves the most common beginner problem of not knowing what to focus on, affordable one-time price and backed by decades of credibility
- Gap: Older production quality compared to newer competitors, less brand recognition among younger audiences who discover BJJ via Instagram or TikTok, no community or accountability component
